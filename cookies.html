Transparency Regarding Local Storage and Privacy

1. What are Cookies?

Cookies and local storage technologies are small text files placed on your browser or device when visiting digital portals. They serve essential technical functions, such as remembering your chosen language, maintaining navigation preferences, and ensuring secure communication.

2. What Technologies Does Ronsel Use?

In alignment with our minimalist and privacy-conscious design philosophy, Ronsel does not operate invasive cross-site advertising trackers or behavioral user profiling cookies. The technologies active on this site fall strictly under:

Category Purpose Duration
Strictly Technical & Functional Preserving your language preference (EN, ES, IT, FR, PT, DE) and filter settings across browser sessions. Session / 1 Year
Third-Party Media (YouTube nocookie) Allowing embedded performance videos to stream on artist profile pages using YouTube's enhanced privacy mode (youtube-nocookie.com), which blocks non-essential tracking cookies until the user initiates playback. Controlled by Third Party

3. Managing and Disabling Cookies

You may freely restrict, block, or delete cookies at any time via your browser preferences:

  • Google Chrome: Settings → Privacy and security → Cookies and other site data.
  • Mozilla Firefox: Options → Privacy & Security → Enhanced Tracking Protection.
  • Apple Safari: Preferences → Privacy → Manage Website Data.
  • Microsoft Edge: Settings → Cookies and site permissions.

Please note that disabling strictly necessary functional cookies may affect certain interface preferences (such as automatic language detection).
