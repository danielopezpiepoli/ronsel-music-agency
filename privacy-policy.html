Data Protection

Privacy Policy

♦

In compliance with Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR)

1. Data Controller

Ronsel: Artist Management & Productions acts as the Data Controller responsible for processing personal data collected through this website and official communication channels.

For any inquiry or exercise of rights concerning your privacy, you may contact our data management desk at: info@ronselmanagement.com.

2. Scope, Purpose & Legal Basis of Processing

We process personal data provided directly by users under the following lawful bases:

Processing Purpose Data Gathered Lawful Basis (GDPR Art. 6)
Booking & Production Inquiries: Answering concert hall programming and casting requests. Name, business email, institution/orchestra name, inquiry details. Pre-contractual measures and legitimate professional interest.
Roster Auditions: Evaluating candidate submissions for potential representation. Name, contact address, curricula vitae, portfolio links, recordings. Consent granted upon voluntary submission.
General Correspondence & Press: Handling information and dossier requests. Name, email address, message body. Consent and legitimate institutional communication.

3. Data Retention Period

Contact and inquiry messages are maintained only for the duration necessary to address your specific artistic or commercial query. Curricula and audition submissions are retained for a maximum of 12 months for seasonal casting reviews, after which they are securely deleted unless active contractual representation is initiated.

4. Data Subject Rights

Under European data protection regulations, you possess the right to:

  • Access: Request confirmation on whether your personal data is being processed.
  • Rectification: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete details.
  • Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"): Demand the deletion of your data when no longer required for initial purposes.
  • Restriction & Objection: Object to processing or request operational limitations.
  • Portability: Obtain your data in a structured, commonly used electronic format.

To exercise any of these prerogatives, submit a formal request accompanied by proof of identity to info@ronselmanagement.com. You likewise retain the right to lodge a complaint with the competent supervisory authority (such as the Spanish Data Protection Agency - AEPD).
